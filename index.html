Official Pool Rules

  • Players: Scott, Ross, Ken, Jim.
  • College: six common games selected by the weekly representative.
  • NFL: all Sunday games plus Sunday Night and Monday Night; Wednesday and Thursday games excluded.
  • College and regular Sunday NFL games = 1 point.
  • Sunday Night NFL and Monday Night NFL = 2 points.
  • Spreads lock Tuesday at midnight.
  • Picks are due Thursday at midnight.
  • Picks remain hidden from other players until after Thursday midnight.
  • Late/no pick: all favorites automatically + a 5-point penalty.
  • College anti-clone: at the deadline, if all four picked the same side, randomly flip one player's pick.
  • Weekly payout: 1st +$24, 2nd −$4, 3rd −$8, 4th −$12.
  • Ties: combine the payouts for tied positions and split equally.
  • Season standings track cumulative W-L and money won/lost.

Pool rules

  • Players: Scott, Ross, Ken, Jim.
  • College: the administrator may select any number of college games each week, with no minimum or maximum.
  • NFL: Sunday games, Sunday Night Football, and Monday Night Football are automatically included; Wednesday and Thursday excluded.
  • Regular games are one point; Sunday Night and Monday Night are two.
  • Late/no pick: favorites automatically, plus a five-point penalty.
  • If all four choose the same college side, one player is randomly flipped.
  • Scott is the administrator unless he transfers the role in the site.
